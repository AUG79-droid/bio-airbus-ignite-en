Turn nature’s strategies into Airbus ideas.

BioLab Airbus is a guided biomimicry experience for teams who want to connect biological intelligence with real industrial, operations and maintenance challenges. Explore a challenge, learn from nature, build a concept and prepare a final pitch.

Stage 2

Explore nature strategies

Use biological models as inspiration. Each strategy is expressed as a function, not as decoration. The goal is to ask: what does nature do here, and how could that become a design principle?

Stage 3

Build your biomimetic canvas

Capture the problem, the biological analogue, the design principle and the Airbus application. The right-hand panel updates live and turns your notes into a pitch-ready concept.
